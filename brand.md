# Reorg Lab

Reorg Lab is an instrument for inspecting a broken assumption: block height alone does not identify history. The interface should read like a carefully annotated engineering experiment, not a trading terminal.

- **Voice:** direct, specific, evidence before claims. English UI. “Saved run” is always visible; local execution is never simulated in the browser.
- **Palette:** cold surface `#f2f5fa`, midnight ink `#14243d`, muted ink `#596b83`, rule `#d3dce8`, cobalt `#285cc7` for navigation and focus. Pine `#176f56` only for canonical/recovered, burnt red `#a94036` for orphaned/diverged, ochre `#886418` for inconclusive. Color always has a written label or symbol.
- **Signature:** a forked-line mark and prominent A/B branch instrument on a quiet dotted measurement surface, with compact hash labels. Structured, cool and technical. No gradients, glow, generic crypto badges, or financial imagery.
- **Typography:** native UI sans (system-ui) for interface and headings; native monospace for hashes, commands and tabular values. No serif editorial headline and no remote font request.
- **Structure:** 1200px maximum content; 24px desktop gutters, 18px mobile. Evidence flows from experiment to projection comparison to controls to reproduction. Timeline connects rather than decorates.
- **Interaction:** native controls, 44px targets, visible 3px focus ring, 100ms hover feedback. Motion only for a small state change, disabled under reduced motion. No autoplay or staged page entrances.
- **Honesty:** hashes, rows, outcomes and counts come from the report. Negative controls have expected inconclusive outcomes and do not become green claims of recovery. Aggregate agreement does not imply row correctness.
